Frequently asked

How is the daily score calculated?
From three classic cycles (physical 23 days, emotional 28 days, intellectual 33 days) that start on your date of birth. The score is their average converted to 0–100 %.

Is it scientifically proven?
No. Controlled studies have not confirmed biorhythms as a predictor. The app is a tool for self-reflection and planning, not medical or professional advice.

How do I restore Premium?
Open Me → Biorhythm Premium → Restore purchase. Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.
