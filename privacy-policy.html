Privacy Policy

Biorhythm (Bioritmus) · effective 26 October 2026

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Dates of birth, names you enter (yourself, your partner, people close to you), your mood journal and settings are stored only on your device.

If you turn on iCloud sync (Premium), the same data is also stored in your private iCloud account so it syncs between your own devices. The developer has no access to it.

Permissions

Photos (add only): used only to save an image of the day that you choose to share.
Notifications: scheduled locally on your device.

Purchases

Biorhythm Premium is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Deleting data

Delete your data in the app or uninstall it to remove all data from the device. iCloud data can be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over.
